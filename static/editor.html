THE BOARD — click a slot, then pick a card

Columns line up: column N blocks column N opposite it, and a column deals the combined power of everything in it. The row nearest the middle is the front row. Click a card you've placed to change it, and hold the middle mouse button on any card to read it.

Click an empty slot on the board to place a card
