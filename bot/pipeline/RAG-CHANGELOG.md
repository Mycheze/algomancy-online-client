# Rules-engine changelog

Every answer logged to `logs/responses.jsonl` carries an `engine_version` field of
the form `DATE.p<prompt-hash>.c<corpus-hash>`:

- **DATE** — hand-bumped `ENGINE_DATE` in `core.py`.
- **p&lt;hash&gt;** — first 8 hex of `sha256(SYSTEM_PROMPT + PRIMER + FOLLOWUP_NOTE)`.
  Flips on any prompt/primer edit (generation behaviour).
- **c&lt;hash&gt;** — first 8 hex of `sha256(data/corpus/algomancy_corpus.jsonl)`. Flips on
  any corpus rebuild (retrieval data).
- **r&lt;hash&gt;** — first 8 hex of the retrieval knobs (`TOP_K`, BM25 params, authority
  boosts, the foundational floor). Flips on any retrieval tuning. Added 2026-07-11;
  answers logged before then have no `r` segment. Without it, a tuning change that
  materially altered what the model saw looked like an unchanged engine in the logs.

To triage feedback by engine, join `feedback.jsonl` → `responses.jsonl` on
`response_id` and filter/group by `engine_version`. Responses logged **before
2026-07-02** predate this field and have `engine_version: null` (or absent) — treat
them as the pre-versioning era (roughly: BM25 + primer landed 2026-06-23, but the
exact prompt/primer wording drifted across that week, so those logs are only
loosely dated by their `ts`).

When you make a real behavioural change: bump `ENGINE_DATE`, and add a row below
recording the new hashes (read them off `python3 -c "import core; print(core.ENGINE_VERSION)"`)
and what changed. That gives every logged answer a precise, filterable lineage.
